## Stated motivation (abstract-level only)

1. **LLM-driven semantic knowledge bases can inherit unsuitable corpus content.** The abstract names outdated, malicious, and privacy-sensitive content as reasons that an SKB must remove specific data.
2. **Semantic encoding, decoding, and reasoning depend on the knowledge base.** Erroneous or sensitive knowledge is therefore a system-level semantic communication concern rather than only an offline corpus-management issue.
3. **Deletion must be efficient and verifiable.** The paper motivates unlearning without retraining from scratch and adds proof because a requester or verifier needs evidence that the specified knowledge was actually removed.

## Scope for this project

This work contributes an audit/verifiability perspective for LLM-enabled semantic knowledge bases. It is not evidence of client-level federated repair for a reconstruction codec, and it should inform verification design only after its full text has been checked.
